Private Celebrations

Celebrate by the ocean at InterContinental Sydney Coogee Beach. Host a baby shower, bridal shower or birthday on the Shutters Terrace overlooking Coogee Beach, in a private room in our Events Centre, or on the Leisure Deck beside the infinity pool.
