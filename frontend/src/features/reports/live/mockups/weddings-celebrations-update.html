Wedding Venues at InterContinental Sydney Coogee Beach

At InterContinental Sydney Coogee Beach, Coogee’s iconic coastline becomes the canvas for your most cherished day.

Gather on our terrace as the sun sets over the ocean, celebrate in the elegance of the Grand Ballroom or dine in the sophistication of Shutters Restaurant, each setting as memorable as the occasion itself. Indoor or outdoor, intimate or grand, every venue is thoughtfully designed to honour your vision and the natural beauty that surrounds it.

This is more than a wedding. It is the beginning of your story, told in one of Sydney’s most breathtaking settings.

Pre-Wedding Experiences

A Day at Eliva Spa – For the Bridal Party. The days before a wedding deserve to be savoured. Treat your bridal party to a private retreat at Eliva Spa, with personalised treatments, a glass of champagne and a curated spa menu.

Coastal Celebrations – For the Groom and His Entourage. A round of golf on one of Sydney’s finest courses, followed by drinks overlooking the eastern coastline.

Private Suite Celebrations. Sweeping ocean views, generous living spaces and attentive personalised service for your group.
